Quick Answer: What is the Yubashakti Scheme Age Limit?

Per the 23 November 2026 West Bengal Cabinet framework, the eligible age bracket for Yubashakti (Bhorsha Karmasuchi) is 21 to 45 years. Applicants under 21 cannot apply, and beneficiaries crossing 45 years are removed from the list.

Frequently Asked Questions (Age Limit FAQ)

Which documents are accepted as age proof?

In West Bengal government schemes, the Madhyamik Admit Card, Birth Certificate, Aadhaar Card, or Voter ID are commonly accepted as proof of age.

Will Yubashakti allowance stop after turning 45?

According to media reports, beneficiaries who turn 45 years old or secure regular employment will be removed from the beneficiary list.
